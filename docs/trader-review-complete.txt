OMXSPI:                       2,871,285        136%      —        —

SURPRISING FINDING: 6-month momentum BEATS regression in raw return!
(7,324% vs 3,802%) — BUT at -58.4% max drawdown vs -34.7%.

Regression advantage:
  - Sharpe: 0.89 vs 0.81 (6mo) — slightly better risk-adjusted
  - Max DD: -34.7% vs -58.4% — MUCH better drawdown protection
  - R² filter removes stocks with noisy/choppy trends

VERDICT: NUANCED — Regression is NOT over-engineered, but for a different
reason than expected. Its value is in RISK CONTROL, not return maximization.
The R² filter acts as a quality gate: it says "yes the stock is going up,
AND the trend is clean/reliable." Simple momentum can't distinguish a
clean trend from a volatile spike-and-crash.

If you can stomach -58% drawdowns, 6-month momentum is actually stronger.
If you want tradeable drawdowns (-35%), regression is the right choice.
